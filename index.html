Hermes

Hermes is a personal assistant application built and used by Scott Barr. It is not offered to the public.

What Hermes does

Each morning Hermes sends its owner a short private briefing: the date, the weather, today's events from his Google Calendar, and upcoming birthdays from his Google Contacts.

How Hermes uses Google data

  • Google Calendar: reads the owner's events to list today's schedule in the briefing.
  • Google Contacts (read-only): reads contact birthdays to list those coming up in the next 7 days.

Data is accessed only for the single account that authorised Hermes, is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer, and is never sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. See the Privacy Policy.
